Predictive race strategy · OpenF1 2024–2026

Predicting F1 lap times, then searching for the fastest pit strategy

A model learns how much slower each lap gets as tyres age; a simulator sums those laps and the pit-stop losses for every possible strategy. This page shows how the models compare, what they learned, and how the simulator holds up against real 2026 races.

race time = Σ predicted lap time + stops × pit loss

Overview

Lap-time models

Every model predicts lap_delta: the fuel-corrected lap time minus that driver's median lap in the race. They are trained on 2024–2025 and scored on the 2026 season they never saw. Lower is better.

What the model learned

SHAP splits every prediction into one contribution per feature. Tyre age dominates; the tyre compound barely moves the prediction, which is the model's main limitation.
